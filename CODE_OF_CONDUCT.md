# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus discussion on the work.
- Protect other people's privacy and never publish their credentials or data.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer contact
or security advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest disclosed
and avoided.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
