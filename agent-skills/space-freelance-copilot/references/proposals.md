## The proposal (under 1,200 characters)
1. First line: their problem in their words (shows you read it). No greeting paragraph, no "I am the best".
2. The closest thing already built (SpaceApp, the catalog sync, the ERP middleware) and what it proves for them.
3. How you would do it: three short steps, the first milestone and its price.
4. One question about their setup that only a reader of the post would ask.
5. Close: "Happy to start with a small paid first milestone."
Answer screening questions directly, from memory. English unless the post is in another language.
