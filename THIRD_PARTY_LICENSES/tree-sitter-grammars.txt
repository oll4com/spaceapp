tree-sitter grammars
Used by: packages/gateway/grammars/*.wasm, loaded by the Space Gateway compression worker for CodeAware
(packages/gateway/src/compression/code-aware). The files are the unmodified WebAssembly builds shipped in the
grammars' npm packages, at the versions Headroom 0.37's parity fixtures were recorded with. The tree-sitter
runtime itself (web-tree-sitter 0.25.10, MIT, Copyright (c) 2018-2024 Max Brunsfeld) is an npm dependency.
